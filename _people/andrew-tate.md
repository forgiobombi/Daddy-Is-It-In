## Biography

Andrew Tate is a British-American former professional kickboxer and internet personality.
He became widely known online in the early 2020s through short-form social media clips,
subscription-based communities, and commentary content.
As of this archive edition, he remains active as an online commentator and entrepreneur.
Within this archive, he is treated as a digital-era case study of pimp-persona aesthetics:
status signaling, control-oriented masculinity branding, and monetized influence tactics.

## Timeline

- **2011–2014:** Wins multiple ISKA kickboxing world titles during his professional career.
- **2016:** Appears on the UK reality TV show *Big Brother*.
- **2022:** Becomes a viral online figure, especially on TikTok, YouTube, and X.
- **2022:** Accounts are restricted on several major social media platforms.
- **2023–2026:** Remains a recurring subject in online culture, platform-policy, and media debates.
